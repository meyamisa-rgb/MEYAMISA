Poem 01

Within Each Breath

I carry you in each breath,
every dawn that finds us apart.
I’m here — my dearest love.
Within the pain, I vow:
all the flowers will bloom.

Poem 02

Dance With the Leaves

It was too late —
she is gone; a little child.
For the rest of your life,
carry on —
dance with the leaves.

Poem 03

Where It Blows

I have chased the wind.
For where it blows —
I, too, shall go.
A sea of flowers still blooming,
where shadows linger.
