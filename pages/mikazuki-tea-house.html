02 · Menu

A quiet menu for a slow tea experience.

Menu commissioned by Kirameki Earth

From a menu commission to an independent branding concept.

Kirameki Earth commissioned me to design the menu for Mikazuki Tea House in Kitahiroshima. That work inspired an independent branding concept based on the tea house’s space, architecture, and atmosphere. The visual direction uses the crescent moon as a quiet measure of time, with circular forms, soft layers, and warm material references.

The translucent rice-paper layer connects the concept to Kitahiroshima, a region known for its rice farming and celebrations of the harvest.
