A Sea of Art

an artist book in two voices

The story

Two people.
Two inner worlds.

Timothée and Madeleine see life through love and art. Their shared pursuit slowly consumes them, until memory becomes the only place where they can still reach one another.

Voice I · Optima

Timothée

precise · inward · blue

I measured the distance between us
in rooms, in seasons, in shades of blue.
Every line was meant to return me to you,
but memory kept moving the shore.

What one forgets,
the other carries.

Voice II · fountain pen

Madeleine

sensory · immediate · warm

You counted what disappeared.
I kept what the water returned:
a yellow light, your unfinished sentence,
the warmth of your hand in mine.

Narrative poetry

The space between their voices.

Beneath the sea
the sun reached down,
its rays touching my skin;
an obscure voice
led me back to you.

We went too far from the ocean
to hear its waves.
Memory began to fade
when we broke our only rule:
not to lose one another.

I closed the door
and left a sea of art behind.
Time changed the place;
the place changed time.

Shared language

Where blue meets warmth.

The book

A memory
you can hold.
