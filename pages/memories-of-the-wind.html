Chasing the Wind — Prologue (Entrance)

Here I stand
Still as the grave at dusk.
She wears my face,
all this ache.

Eyes, a dying seed.
Hush the crowd,
my colors bleed.
The weight of seeing all.
Flowers breathe.

Feared is the abyss
that dwells within me.
The soul that sought the wind
and skies so true,
nor do I find love within myself.

Unknown light.
No more sorrow —
I cast off the weight,
fire that frees.

No longer do I crave
the death that whispers.
I feel the pain,
as artists do.

In every lifetime,
as life burned,
a flower unfurls —
life amidst death.

I have chased the wind.
For where it blows —
I, too, shall go.

A grin that knows
it costs to walk free.
A sea of flowers still blooming,
where shadows linger —
and claim their hour.
