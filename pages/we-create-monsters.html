Tim Schmeer Lab · Asemic writing · 2022

We Create Monsters

These twenty signs began with fragments of writing. Letterforms were cut apart, shifted, and recombined until language lost its readable meaning and began to behave like a creature. Each form sits between glyph, body, and imaginary alphabet: unreadable, but full of posture, rhythm, and character.

Developed during We Create Monsters, a workshop on typographic forms of expression with Tim Schmeer at the Peter Behrens School of Arts.
